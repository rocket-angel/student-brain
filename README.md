# Student Brain

The empty skeleton of your brain — the AI-Native Year, month one.

This repo is deliberately empty. It gives you the SHAPE of what you will build:

    db/founding/     the founding of your brain's database — arrives in lesson 10
    db/organ/        the walls and the proof suite — lessons 17 and 18
    organ/           the five commands your brain runs on — lessons 6 to 47
    identity/        who your brain is when it answers — lessons 38 and 39
    brain-folder/    (optional home for the folder your brain reads — lesson 4)

Every file that belongs in these folders is given to you inside the course,
lesson by lesson, each one with the record of what was run to prove it works.
By the end of month one this tree is full, proven against your own live
database, and backed up — and the months after keep building in the same home.

## Getting started

1. Download this repo: Code → Download ZIP (or clone it).
2. Unzip it as `~/brain` — every path in the course is relative to this folder.
3. Open lesson 1 and build.

Nothing here spends money or touches a database until the course gives you the
files and you run them with your own keys — and your spend cap goes on (lesson
41) before the first lesson that spends.
